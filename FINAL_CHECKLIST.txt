                     AUTOMATIC CALCULATIONS
================================================================================

✅ GST CALCULATION (17%)
   - Applied to all invoices
   - Formula: Subtotal × 0.17
   - Real-time calculation as items change
   - Display in invoice summary

✅ PROFIT MARGIN
   - Tours: (Sell Price - Base Price) / Base Price × 100
   - Automatic on package creation
   - Displayed in package list

✅ TOTAL REVENUE
   - Formula: Subtotal + GST - Discount
   - Updates in real-time
   - Shown on invoice

✅ LEAD SCORE
   - Auto-calculated on creation
   - Based on budget
   - Used for prioritization
